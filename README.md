# Prompt Engineering – Task 2 (2026)
## AI Content Marketing using UGC Ads

This repository contains my submission for Task 2 of the Prompt Engineering Internship (2026) by Future Interns.

In this task, I created AI-powered prompts to generate UGC-style ad content and a cinematic short product advertisement video for a real product.

---

## 📌 Project Details

**Product:** SoundBeat Pro Wireless Earbuds  
**Category:** Consumer Electronics  
**Use Case:** Short-Form Social Media Ads  

**Target Audience:**
- Students  
- Commuters  
- Gym Users  

---

## 🎯 Objective

- Generate UGC-style ad scripts
- Create multiple hooks
- Follow Problem → Solution → CTA framework
- Optimize content for Reels, Ads, and Shorts
- Generate premium product visuals and short video ads

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- ChatGPT – Prompt design and ad scripts

## 🎨 Image Generation Prompt (Leonardo AI)

```
A hyper-realistic ultra-premium 3D render of sleek black wireless earbuds and charging case, floating in mid-air, lid open, earbuds glowing softly, dramatic studio lighting, soft golden rim light, glossy reflections, cinematic shadows, luxury tech advertisement style, dark gradient background with subtle light streaks, shallow depth of field, macro lens look, ultra-detailed textures, photorealistic, 8K quality, product photography for high-end brand campaign
```

---

## 🎬 Video Generation Prompt (Pika)

```
Create a cinematic luxury product advertisement video using this image of black wireless earbuds.

Slow smooth 360-degree rotation of the earbuds and charging case.

Soft glowing rim light and subtle golden light streaks moving in the background.

Add gentle floating motion and slight camera zoom in.

Show realistic reflections on the surface and glossy texture.

Animate soft soundwave particles and light pulses around the earbuds.

Professional commercial lighting, premium tech brand style.

Background stays dark and elegant with cinematic depth.

Text overlay (smooth fade-in animation):
"30H Battery"
"Noise Cancellation"
"Deep Bass"

Final text:
"Upgrade Your Sound"

Ultra-smooth motion, no shaking, no distortion.

High-end advertisement look, Apple-style product launch aesthetic.

Aspect ratio 9:16, duration 8–10 seconds, 4K quality, social media ad style.
use clear readable English text, no distorted letters, sharp typography, minimal modern font
```
